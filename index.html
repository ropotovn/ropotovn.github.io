Hermes Workspace

A personal automation tool for one user.

Hermes Workspace is a private, single-user automation utility. It connects to the Google Workspace APIs of its owner (Gmail, Google Drive, Google Sheets, Google Calendar and Google Docs) in order to read and write the owner's own documents, spreadsheets and calendar events on the owner's behalf. There are no other users, no sign-up, and no public service.

What it does

  • Reads and writes spreadsheets owned by the account holder (marketplace analytics workbooks).
  • Reads and writes documents owned by the account holder.
  • Reads the account holder's own calendar to prepare a daily schedule.
  • Reads and sends email from the account holder's own mailbox for notifications and drafts.

Data handling

All data stays within the owner's own Google account and the owner's own computer. Nothing is transmitted to third parties, nothing is sold, and no analytics or advertising is involved. See the Privacy Policy for details.
